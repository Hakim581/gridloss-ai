# Competition notes

**Problem:** Technical line losses are expected, but a feeder-level gap beyond modeled loss warrants investigation. Missing data and meter failure can mimic this gap.

**Innovation:** Sensor-based energy conservation anchors the result; statistical and ML behavior models prioritize meter checks; readable explanations expose why each label was produced.

**Responsible wording:** UI labels are Normal, Low Risk, Medium Risk, High Risk, and Requires Inspection. The product uses “Non-Technical Loss Risk,” “Unexplained Energy,” “Anomalous Consumption Pattern,” and “Inspection Recommended.” It does not call a customer a thief.

**Pilot path:** validate feeder metering and phase mapping; calibrate line impedances and load profiles; ingest actual AMI quality codes; establish seasonal and weather baselines; test on historical known faults; involve utility engineers in threshold selection; require human review and documented inspection outcomes.
